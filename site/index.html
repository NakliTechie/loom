INMOS T800s emulated by pahihu's t4, compiled to WebAssembly, one per Worker, in lockstep on one virtual clock (10 instructions per microsecond; link messages move at quantum barriers), so every run is deterministic. Drop a configured bootable with its .map and the fabric wires itself to match.

The 1990 occam 2 toolset (D7205A) runs inside the transputer. It is not shipped here — see why. Drop your copy of d7205a.tar.gz and build compiles, links, configures and collects an .occ into a bootable.
